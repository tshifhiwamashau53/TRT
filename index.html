PASSENGER HELP

Answers before you travel

Is the timetable live?

No. The D23–D26 information here is reference timetable data. Always check official TRT notices before travelling.

How do I find a route?

Use Plan a trip for an origin and destination, or select D23, D24, D25 or D26 in the schedule finder.

Can I save my regular route?

Yes. Select the star on a route card. Saved routes are stored locally on your device.

Where can I get official TRT information?

Use the official TRT website link in the service notice or contact TRT directly.
